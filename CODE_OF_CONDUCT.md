# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and scientifically rigorous collaboration environment. Contributors must communicate constructively, document limitations honestly, protect patient privacy, and avoid overstating clinical claims. Harassment, discrimination, disclosure of restricted information, or manipulation of analyses to obtain favorable results is not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect contributors, patients, or scientific integrity.
